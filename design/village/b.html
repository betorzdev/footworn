B · One house per visit

Each site is a floating island at golden hour; the village on it grows through the day, one cottage at a time. At UTC midnight it is empty again.
  • Page = a quarter of the village, with its name and count.
  • Pageview = one cottage (count them: the quarter is a pictogram chart).
  • Roof colour = where that visit came from (referrer).
  • Lit window = that load was used; dark = only opened.
  • Event = a balloon over the quarter. Live = a cottage dropping in.

Drag to turn · wheel to zoom · click an island or a quarter.
