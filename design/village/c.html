C · Clock square

Each site is a snowed-in village round a clock tower on a winter night. The square is the day; the ring of houses the pages; the palisade the ways in.
  • Hour = one of 24 lamp posts round the square (clockwise from midnight at the top); height = pageviews in that hour; the ring on it = yesterday’s; the lit one is now.
  • Page = a house in the ring, busiest at twelve o’clock; floors = today’s pageviews; warm windows = used.
  • Referrer = a gate in the palisade, as wide as its arrivals.
  • Visit = a villager walking in through the gate, across the square, home.
  • Event = a spark burst over that roof.
