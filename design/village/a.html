A · Tower village

Each site is a village at dusk; its houses stand in one row along the main street, busiest first.
  • Page = a tower house; floors = today’s pageviews (same scale everywhere, so heights compare like bars).
  • Warm windows, from the ground up = share of loads used; cool = only opened.
  • Referrer = a road into the gate; its width = today’s arrivals.
  • Visit = a villager with a lantern in the road’s colour, walking to the house and in.
  • Event = a paper lantern rising from that roof.
