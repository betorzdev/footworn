What Footworn counts

Footworn is the visit counter of the site that sent you here. It measures the site’s audience and nothing else.

It keeps

  • The page you opened, and the site you came from (its hostname only).
  • Your browser and system families (Chrome, Firefox… Windows, Android…), the width of your window, and your language.
  • Your country, read at the edge from your connection.
  • Actions on the page the site chose to count (which screen you opened, a share), by name.

Each of these is stored as a count per day and shown only aggregated, and is deleted after 25 months.

It never keeps

  • Cookies, local storage or any identifier in your browser.
  • Your IP address or your full browser signature. To tell a repeat visit from a new one within the same day, both are hashed with a random key that is destroyed every night; the hash is never stored with a visit and cannot be reversed afterwards.
  • Your path through the site, or anything that links one day’s visits to another’s.

Nothing is shared with anyone, nothing is used for anything but counting, and nothing is tracked across sites. Footworn is open source: the site’s privacy notice links to its code.
